# Paper-related material

`references_wfiot_revision.bib` contains the bibliography additions used for the
camera-ready revision, including the public dataset, MLPerf Tiny / edge benchmarking
references, the BME688 edge-smoke study, and the preliminary IEEE MeditCom 2026
FireTransformer paper.

The preliminary paper must be explicitly distinguished from the WF-IoT contribution:

> C. Matta, D. D. Giusto, and M. Anedda, “FireTransformer: An Encoder-Only
> Transformer for Early Wildfire Detection in IoT Sensor Networks,” 2026 IEEE
> International Mediterranean Conference on Communications and Networking
> (MeditCom), pp. 1–6, 2026.

The WF-IoT revision extends the preliminary study through horizon-based warning,
leakage-controlled full cross-node evaluation, repeated-run analysis, horizon
sensitivity, and explicit computational/energy characterization.

The final early-warning evaluation defines a physical onset only by a `0 -> 1`
transition of the fire annotation. Coverage and lead time are event-level quantities
and use acquisition-file identity to distinguish events across separate recordings.

The WF-IoT experimental comparison uses FT-64, FT-128, BiLSTM-BCE and BiLSTM-FL.
FT-32 is not part of the final experimental comparison.

No estimated performance or energy result should be copied into the paper. Values in
the manuscript must be generated by the scripts in this repository or otherwise be
clearly identified as analytical quantities.
